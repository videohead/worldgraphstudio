# Why does this project exist

Hi! I am a multi-faceted creative professional. At various points in my career(s), I have been an digital artist, writer, filmmaker, video producer, web developer, production manager, camera person and about 20 other things. I discovered AI filmmmaking through some friends, including the Machine Cinema group and was intrigued. But there was a lot missing from the AI Gold Rush and the never ending FOMO stream of new models and tools. Most of what I saw was missing was a core focus on story.

WordPress is a web platform used across the world. There are more WordPress websites out there than any other kinds of website, it's kind of a juggernaut. And it's GOOD at understanding content - it's a content management system. It's also story aware, and AI-aware - there are agents and MCPs built into it.

Story graph and plot analysis graphs have been around for a while - the 1950s? It's not something I invented. I've enjoyed working in this space for a while, and it's pretty much the perfect intersection between how we humans understand the world and its stories, and how AI wants to perceive ideas and concepts.

So my original idea was just to marry my project sketches in WordPress with ComfyUI, and see what I could write and generate for free on my crappy desktop, run it through some story graph analysis tools and see what happened. Pretty quickly, I found that I needed a lot more tools, so I built them in. The idea of keeping it open - get in and get out for free - was a core part of the effort. I promise to never change that.

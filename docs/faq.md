# FAQ

## Do I need AI?

No.

World Graph Studio works without AI.

## Do I need ComfyUI?

No.

Use any generation tool.

## Do I need paid AI credits?

No.

World Graph Studio itself never requires credits.

Some third-party AI services may have their own usage costs.

## Do I need a website?

No.

Most users run World Graph Studio locally using WordPress Studio or LocalWP.

## Who owns the content?

You do.

Projects, assets, stories, and exports remain under your control.

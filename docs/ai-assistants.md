# AI Assistants

AI assistance is optional. World Graph Studio remains useful for authoring,
planning, relationships, continuity, assets, import, and export without an AI
provider.

## What AI can help with

The AI Editor can use the current record and related Story Graph context to
support analysis, planning, continuity review, story structure, and production
questions. World Graph Studio also includes more than 50 focused specialist
profiles for creative and production-oriented advice.

AI output is a proposal, not source material or a final creative decision. It
can misunderstand context, invent details, repeat stereotypes, or produce text
that is not suitable for publication. Review and edit every result.

## Before using AI

An administrator must configure and publish a supported LLM Connection. This
is separate from an ordinary consumer chat subscription: API access may need a
different account, credential, and billing arrangement.

When you use a hosted provider, relevant prompts and Story Graph context leave
your WordPress site. Do not send confidential material, personal information,
or protected work until you have reviewed the provider's terms, privacy,
retention, and model-training policies.

## Working effectively

Give the assistant a specific task and state the kind of response you want.
For example:

- “Identify continuity conflicts in this Scene and list the records involved.”
- “Suggest three ways to strengthen the Character's decision without changing
  the established ending.”
- “Turn this Scene into a preliminary shot-planning checklist.”

Ask it to cite the Story Graph records behind its conclusions, then verify
those records yourself. Keep the creative brief, constraints, and intended
audience explicit.

## AI is not media generation

Textual AI assistance and image, audio, or video generation use different
Connections and Templates. Configuring an LLM does not automatically configure
a media provider. See [Generation Workflows](generation-workflows.md) for that
process.
